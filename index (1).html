The Story

From raw books to living memory

Imagine a world where every chapter, prophecy, map, and family tree is not simply stored—but deeply understood. Citadel RAG does exactly that. It begins with PDF ingestion and transforms a sprawling fantasy universe into a structured knowledge base with context, metadata, and retrieval pathways.

Workflow

How the intelligence engine works

01

Ingest the source

Books, maps, illustrations, and documents are parsed from PDFs and transformed into usable text and image assets.

→
02

Understand the content

Vision models describe illustrations, while text is segmented by chapter, paragraph, and POV boundaries to preserve meaning.

→
03

Chunk and embed

Recursive semantic chunking creates precise knowledge units, then converts them into vector embeddings for retrieval.

→
04

Store and retrieve

Metadata-rich embeddings are stored in Pinecone and queried by similarity, returning relevant historical evidence and images.

→
05

Respond with evidence

The AI generates grounded responses with citations to book, chapter, and page-level sources for transparent answers.

Workflow of the system

A proper intelligence pipeline works in layers: collect, interpret, index, retrieve, and answer responsibly.

1

Ingest

PDFs and worldbook files enter the system from the source corpus.

2

Parse

Text, pages, diagrams, maps, and illustrations are extracted with structure intact.

3

Caption

Visual content is understood through AI vision and enriched with descriptive metadata.

4

Chunk

Long narratives are split by paragraph, sentence, and context to preserve meaning without fragmentation.

5

Embed

Each chunk is converted into vector embeddings and stored in a semantic search layer.

6

Serve

User questions trigger retrieval and generation, followed by source-backed, cited responses.
